# Incident Response Workflow
1. Establish impact, timeline, affected services, and severity.
2. Preserve evidence; avoid speculative changes.
3. Assign incident lead and technical owners.
4. Prefer reversible mitigation with explicit approval for production actions.
5. Verify recovery with health checks and user journeys.
6. Identify root cause after stabilization.
7. Create corrective actions, owners, and due dates.
8. Produce a factual incident report without exposing secrets or personal data.
